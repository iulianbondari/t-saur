# Code of conduct

T-saur is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or reviews, is expected to keep the following.

## Expected

* Be respectful and precise. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
* Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
* Keep discussions on the project: the format, the code, the documentation, the measurements.
* Report defects with what you observed, what you expected and how to reproduce it; report
  security issues privately as described in `SECURITY.md`.
* Credit sources and prior work; state clearly when something is an estimate or has not been
  verified.

## Not accepted

* Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (Iulian Bondari) handles reports, in confidence, through the private channels
listed in `SECURITY.md` or a direct message on GitHub. Consequences range from a request to
change behaviour to removal from the project's spaces, depending on the severity and whether it
recurs. Decisions are explained to the people involved.
